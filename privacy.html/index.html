Privacy Policy

Multi Uploader · Effective November 14, 2026

Multi Uploader is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. Its operator uses it to post their own videos to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When the operator signs in with TikTok, the app receives only what the approved permissions allow:

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is signed in.
  • video.upload and video.publish: permission to upload videos the operator selects and publish them to that same account.

The app reads the video files and captions the operator chooses on their own computer and sends them to TikTok, YouTube and Instagram through each platform's official API.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are saved only on the operator's own computer, so the operator doesn't have to sign in every time. The app has no server, database, analytics, advertising or tracking. No data is sold, shared with or sent to any third party other than the platform a video is being posted to.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the operator's computer until deleted. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, which makes the saved TikTok token invalid. Videos that were posted can be deleted on each platform as usual.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
